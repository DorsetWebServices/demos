Local Craftsmanship

Serving Mampitts Lane & The Greater Shaftesbury Community

Nestled in the bustling neighborhood of Mampitts Lane, Shaftesbury, A & P Hare Limited was founded on a simple belief: every community deserves extraordinary, honest bread.

In an era of rushed factory loafs, we take step-by-step pride in traditional methods. From stoneground Wessex flours to our 10-year-old sourdough starter, every batch is mixed, shaped, and scored by human hands right here in North Dorset.

Whether you are stopping by after the morning school run, grabbing lunch near Gold Hill, or seeking artisanal sourdough for your weekend gathering, A & P Hare Limited offers a warm Dorset welcome and unmatched quality.
