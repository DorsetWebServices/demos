Shaftesbury's High Street Scaffolding & Access Authority

Headquartered directly on High Street, Shaftesbury (SP7), Boiler Zone Installations provides tailored scaffolding solutions designed specifically for the unique architectural terrain of North Dorset. From narrow steep access lanes around historic Gold Hill to modern residential builds in Enmore Green and St James.
