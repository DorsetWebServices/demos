Welcome to Eco-Flame Limited

Harmonising Natural Heritage with Modern Outdoor Luxury

Operating from our studio on historic Bell Street in Shaftesbury, Eco-Flame Limited represents the pinnacle of high-end British garden design and sustainable outdoor architecture. We cater to homeowners across North Dorset and Wiltshire who demand craftsmanship, environmental responsibility, and understated elegance.

From bespoke fire tables and clean-burn outdoor stoves to native planting schemes, limestone paving, and bespoke timber structures—we turn outdoor spaces into usable year-round havens.
