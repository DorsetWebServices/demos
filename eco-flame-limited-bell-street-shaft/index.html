Serving Bell Street, Shaftesbury & the Surrounding North Dorset Countryside

At Eco-Flame Limited, located conveniently on Bell Street in Shaftesbury, we take immense pride in enhancing the natural beauty of Dorset’s unique landscapes. From the iconic heights near Gold Hill to rural properties across the Blackmore Vale, our landscape designs respect local heritage while introducing cutting-edge sustainable practices.

Operating throughout Shaftesbury, Gillingham, Motcombe, Fontmell Magna, Semley, Tisbury, and Blandford Forum, our team is deeply familiar with North Dorset's soil profiles, local conservation guidelines, and regional stone materials.

Whether you own a Grade II listed cottage requiring sensitive stone retaining walls or a modern hilltop residence seeking clean-lined porcelain paving and zero-emission outdoor fireplaces, Eco-Flame Limited delivers unrivaled local expertise.
