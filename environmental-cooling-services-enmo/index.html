Combining Traditional Dorset Carpentry with Modern Eco-Cooling Design

Situated at the foot of Gold Hill in historic Enmore Green, Shaftesbury, Environmental Cooling Services offers a unique approach to carpentry. We specialize in working with timber as a natural thermal regulator for residential and commercial buildings across North Dorset and the Blackmore Vale.

As summers become warmer, traditional air conditioning can be energy-intensive and visually jarring on historic Dorset homes. Our joinery team crafts architectural timber solutions—such as solar louvres, brise-soleil, cedar shading, and breathable clad extensions—that drastically reduce interior heat without electrical cooling.

Whether you require a bespoke oak frame in Motcombe, timber cladding in Fontmell Magna, or custom energy-efficient window shutters in central Shaftesbury, our workshop delivers unrivaled master joinery tailored to the local landscape.
