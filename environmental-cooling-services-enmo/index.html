Our Specialisms

Environmental Carpentry & High-End Joinery

We combine time-honoured Dorset woodworking techniques with passive microclimate control—delivering aesthetic elegance and natural cooling performance.

Solar Brise-Soleil & Shading

Precision-engineered timber louvres and exterior shading fins. Reduces internal solar heat gain by up to 75% during hot Shaftesbury summers without sacrificing natural light.

  • Fixed & adjustable timber fins
  • Western Red Cedar & Siberian Larch

Green Oak Framed Structures

Hand-crafted green oak porches, garden pavilions, shaded pergolas, and oak-framed extensions. Built with traditional draw-pegged mortise and tenon joints.

  • Structurally engineered timber
  • Sourced from sustainable UK forests

Ventilated Timber Cladding

High-performance architectural rainscreen cladding systems. Designed to create a thermal convective buffer zone that keeps external warmth out and insulates in winter.

  • Shadow-gap & secret-fix profiles
  • Enhanced thermal envelope efficiency

Custom Outdoor Living Canopies

Bespoke open-air timber canopies with integrated slatted roofs, passive airflow channels, and climate protection for Dorset country residences.

  • Integrated guttering & lighting tracks
  • Weatherproof oil finishes

Heritage Joinery & Restoration

Expert repair and replacement of timber structural elements, sash windows, heavy doors, and period features in listed Shaftesbury and Enmore Green properties.

  • Grade I & II Listed experience
  • Match-existing moulding profiles

Architectural Interior Joinery

Custom hardwood staircases, timber ceiling baffle systems for acoustic and thermal comfort, fitted alcoves, and bespoke internal acoustic panelling.

  • English Walnut, Oak & Ash finishes
  • Millimetre-perfect site fitting
Local Heritage & Eco Engineering

Rooted in Enmore Green, Serving the Shaftesbury Hilltop Microclimate

Situated at the base of Shaftesbury's historic Gold Hill and Cranborne Chase area, Environmental Cooling Services brings a specialized approach to carpentry. Enmore Green homes experience unique sun exposure and hilltop weather fluctuations.

Rather than relying solely on energy-intensive air conditioning, our master carpenters construct passive architectural timber solutions—such as cedar brise-soleil systems, oak shade pergolas, and ventilated timber facades—that naturally lower indoor temperatures while elevating your property's value.
