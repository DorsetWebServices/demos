More Than Just a Shop: The Heart of Motcombe

Nestled just minutes from the historic hilltop town of Shaftesbury and the charming parish of St James, Motcombe Community Shop stands as a vibrant testament to local spirit and sustainable rural living in North Dorset.

When traditional rural amenities began closing across the UK, the residents of Motcombe rallied together to establish a community-owned retail shop and café. Today, our dedicated team of volunteers and shop managers stock a curated selection of Dorset cheeses, local farm-fresh milk, regional meats, craft preserves, and everyday pantry items.

Whether you are taking a countryside walk near Gold Hill, commuting through St James, or looking for a warm espresso and catch-up with neighbors, our doors are open 7 days a week to welcome you.
