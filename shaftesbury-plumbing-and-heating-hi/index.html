Serving High Street, SP7 & The Surrounding North Dorset Countryside

Operating from our base on Shaftesbury’s historic High Street, we understand the unique plumbing and heating demands of Dorset’s architectural landscape. From Grade-II listed cottages near Gold Hill and St James to modern developments on the edge of town, our engineers combine traditional care with cutting-edge technology.
