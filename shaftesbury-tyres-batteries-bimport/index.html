Why is an Artisan Bakery Called "Shaftesbury Tyres & Batteries"?

If you walk along Bimport in Shaftesbury, UK, just a stone’s throw from the world-famous Gold Hill and Trinity Church, you'll spot an old historic sign reading Shaftesbury Tyres & Batteries. When our master baker transformed this iconic 1950s motor workshop into a neighborhood bakery, locals begged us to keep the quirky name.

Today, we are proud to be North Dorset’s favorite artisan bakery. We recharge the people of Shaftesbury with high-grade espresso, long-fermentation sourdough loaves, and delicate pastries instead of spark plugs and fan belts!
